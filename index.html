Privacy Policy for Digital Interest Book

Effective date: August 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Digital Interest Book ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our mobile application.

By using Digital Interest Book, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the App, we may collect the following information:

  • Account information: information associated with your account, such as your email address, phone number, display name, and Firebase user identifier (UID).
  • Authentication information: information needed to sign you in, including Google Sign-In information or mobile-number/OTP authentication information.
  • App data: information that you choose to enter or store in the App, such as customer records, transaction/ledger information, amounts, dates, notes, and related business records.
  • Technical information: limited technical information that may be provided by Firebase or Android/Google services to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the App.

2. How We Use Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Authenticate your identity and provide secure sign-in.
  • Provide the App's ledger, customer, and record-management features.
  • Store and synchronize your data across supported devices and sessions.
  • Send authentication messages such as OTPs when required for sign-in.
  • Protect the App against fraud, abuse, unauthorized access, and security threats.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the App.
  • Comply with applicable laws and legal obligations.

3. Firebase and Google Services

The App uses services provided by Google Firebase and, where applicable, Google Sign-In. These services may process information on our behalf to provide authentication, database storage, application functionality, security, and related services.

Authentication and application data may be stored using Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore.

Google's handling of information is also governed by Google's applicable privacy policies and service terms.

4. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Data may be stored on infrastructure operated by Google Firebase and may be protected by authentication and access-control mechanisms.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers that are necessary to operate the App, such as Google Firebase and Google authentication services. We may also disclose information when required by law, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, security, and integrity of our users or the App.

6. Your Data and Account

You are responsible for the information you enter into the App. You should avoid entering information that is unnecessary for the App's intended functionality.

You can delete your Digital Interest Book account directly from the account settings in the App by using the available "Delete Account" option.

Account deletion is intended to remove your account and associated application data stored for your account, subject to information that we are legally required to retain.

Some information may be retained when necessary to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent fraud or abuse, or maintain security. Any information retained for these reasons will be kept only for as long as required for the applicable legal, security, or other legitimate purpose and will be deleted when the applicable retention requirement ends.

7. Account Deletion

How to request account deletion

Users can request deletion of their Digital Interest Book account and associated data directly from the App:

  1. Open the Digital Interest Book app.
  2. Sign in to your account.
  3. Open your Account or Profile settings.
  4. Select "Delete Account".
  5. Confirm the account deletion when prompted.

After the deletion request is confirmed, the account and associated application data will be deleted, subject to data that we are legally required or permitted to retain.

Data deleted: account information and associated application data stored for the account, including profile information, customer records, transaction/ledger records, notes, and other user-created application data, where applicable.

Data that may be retained: information that is required to be retained for legal compliance, fraud prevention, security, dispute resolution, or other legitimate legal purposes.

Any retained information will be kept only for the period required for the applicable purpose and will be deleted when that retention period ends.

8. Children's Privacy

Digital Interest Book is intended for general users and is not specifically directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without appropriate legal authorization or parental involvement where required by applicable law.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us improperly, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.
